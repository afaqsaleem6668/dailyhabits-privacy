Privacy Policy

App Name: Daily Habits: Streak Tracker
Application ID: com.dailyhabits.streakapp
Last Updated: November 24, 2026
Core Privacy Principle: Daily Habits: Streak Tracker is designed to be 100% offline. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal data, habits, or usage logs. Everything you track stays solely on your physical device.

1. Information We Collect

We collect zero personal data. Specifically:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • No Device Information or Identifiers: We do not collect advertising IDs, IMEI numbers, IP addresses, or MAC addresses.
  • No Location Data: The app does not access your GPS, coarse location, or fine location.
  • No Financial or Payment Information: The application is completely free and contains no in-app purchases or billing libraries.

2. How Your Habit Data Is Stored

All data you create inside the app—including habit titles, completion dates, streaks, and timestamps—is stored locally in your device's private sandboxed internal storage using standard Android local storage (SharedPreferences).

This data never leaves your device. It is never transmitted over the Internet or uploaded to any remote server or cloud database.

3. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Daily Habits: Streak Tracker contains:

  • No Advertising SDKs: No third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob, Unity, or Meta).
  • No Analytics SDKs: No tracking SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics, Flurry, or Mixpanel).
  • No Crash-Reporting Trackers: No external telemetry or telemetry beacons.

4. Device Permissions

This application requests zero sensitive Android permissions. It does not require access to your Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Photo Library, Location, or Storage.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our application is safe for users of all ages, including children under the age of 13. Because the application does not collect any personal data from anyone, it complies fully with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data resides exclusively on your local device:

  • You can clear all habit records at any time using the Reset All Data feature in the in-app Settings menu.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently erases all stored habit data from your device immediately.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via your Google Play developer contact email.
